## Partner Blood Banks

Partner Blood Banks are external blood banks or trusted partner organizations that supply blood units to the system. This module helps the team keep partner details in one place and use them consistently in the Blood Bank workflow.

![Partner Blood Banks listing page](/screens/master-data/partner-bloodbank/Partner_BloodBank_Listing.png)

## Overview

The Partner Blood Banks module is used to manage all external blood bank partners in one place.

From this page, users can:

- view all partner blood banks in a table
- add a new partner blood bank
- edit an existing partner blood bank
- mark a partner as active or inactive
- delete a partner blood bank when it is no longer needed

This module keeps partner blood bank information organized and helps staff use the correct partner record during the blood intake workflow.

## Purpose

The main purpose of this module is to store and maintain partner blood bank details in a standard format.

Instead of entering partner details manually each time, staff can select a saved partner blood bank from the list. This reduces mistakes and keeps the workflow consistent.

## Page Layout

The Partner Blood Banks page shows a table with the main details needed for daily work.

The page also includes an **Add Partner Blood Bank** button for creating a new entry.

Each row contains action buttons that let users edit or delete the record as needed.

## Field Guide

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Code** | Auto-generated code for the partner blood bank. |
| **Name** | Partner blood bank name. |
| **Contact Person** | The person to contact at that partner blood bank. |
| **Phone / Email** | Contact details for communication. |
| **Address / City** | Location details for the partner blood bank. |
| **Status** | Shows whether the partner blood bank is **Active** or **Inactive**. |


![Partner Blood Banks actions](/screens/master-data/partner-bloodbank/Partner_BloodBank_Actions.png)

## Actions Available

Each partner blood bank row supports the following actions:

- **Edit** - update partner details
- **Delete** - remove the partner blood bank if it is no longer required
- **Status** - review whether the partner is active or inactive

These actions help maintain the partner list without leaving the page.

## Add Partner Blood Bank

Use this option when you want to create a new partner blood bank entry.

### Steps to Add Partner Blood Bank

1. Open the **Partner Blood Banks** page.
2. Click **Add Partner Blood Bank**.
3. Enter the partner blood bank name.
4. Enter the contact person name.
5. Enter the phone number or email address.
6. Enter the address and city details.
7. Review the details carefully.
8. Save the record.

After saving, the new partner blood bank appears in the listing and becomes available for use where applicable.

![Add Partner Blood Bank form](/screens/master-data/partner-bloodbank/Partner_BloodBank_Add.png)

## Edit Partner Blood Bank

Use the edit option when an existing partner blood bank needs to be corrected or updated.

### Steps to Edit Partner Blood Bank

1. Find the required partner blood bank in the list.
2. Click the **Edit** button.
3. Update the required fields.
4. Review the changes.
5. Save the record.

Editing is useful when contact details change, the address needs correction, or the status must be updated.

![Edit Partner Blood Bank form](/screens/master-data/partner-bloodbank/Partner_BloodBank_Edit.png)

## Status Handling

Partner Blood Banks can be maintained with two status values:

- **Active**
- **Inactive**

### Active

An active partner blood bank is available for use in daily workflows.

### Inactive

An inactive partner blood bank is kept in the system for reference, but it is not meant to be used for new operational entries.

This helps the team keep only valid and current partner records available for work.

![Partner Blood Bank status handling](/screens/master-data/partner-bloodbank/Partner_BloodBank_status.png)

## Delete Behavior

If a partner blood bank is no longer needed, it can be deleted from the module.

### What happens when a partner blood bank is deleted

- a confirmation prompt appears first
- the user must confirm the action
- after confirmation, the record is removed from the listing

This helps prevent accidental removal of partner records that may still be needed.

![Delete Partner Blood Bank confirmation](/screens/master-data/partner-bloodbank/Partner_BloodBank_Delete.png)

## Used In

Partner Blood Banks are used in the blood intake workflow when the team receives blood from an external partner.

When a blood unit comes from another blood bank, staff can select the partner blood bank from this list instead of typing the details manually. This keeps the source information clean and consistent.

This module is also useful for checking which partner supplied a blood unit before it entered the system.

![Partner Blood Bank usage in other workflows](/screens/master-data/partner-bloodbank/Partner_BloodBank_Usage.png)

## Important Notes

- Keep partner blood bank names clear and easy to identify.
- Always maintain correct contact details for communication.
- Enter the address and city carefully so the partner can be identified easily.
- Mark unused partner blood banks as inactive instead of leaving them active.
- Delete a partner blood bank only when it is no longer needed.
- The partner blood bank selected during blood unit creation becomes part of the blood unit record.
