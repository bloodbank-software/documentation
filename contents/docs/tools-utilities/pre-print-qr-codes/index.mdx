## Pre-Print QR Codes
The Pre-Print QR Codes page is used to generate QR codes in advance before they are assigned to blood units.

This page helps staff prepare QR codes in bulk and review them before printing.

![Pre-Print QR Codes overview](/screens/tools-utilities/pre-print-qr-codes/PrePrintQR_Overview.png)


## Overview

The Pre-Print QR Codes page allows users to create QR codes before blood unit allocation.

Users can configure how many QR codes to generate, set duplicate count if needed, review the generated list, and print the QR sheets when required.

## Purpose

The main purpose of this page is to support advance QR generation for blood bank operations.

It is useful when QR codes need to be prepared ahead of collection, stock handling, or unit assignment workflows.

## Page Layout

The page is divided into two main parts:

- a QR generation area at the top
- a QR list area below it

The generation area is used to set the required values before creating QR codes.

The list area shows generated QR codes in tabs for easy review.

## Generate QR Codes

To generate QR codes, the user enters the required values and starts the generation process.

The user can set:

- quantity
- duplicate count

After the values are entered, the page generates the QR codes for later use.

![Pre-Print QR Codes generate and print](/screens/tools-utilities/pre-print-qr-codes/PrePrintQR_Generate&Print.png)

## Camp Selection

Camp selection is used when the QR codes need to be linked to a camp.

This helps staff group QR codes by camp and keep the QR batches easier to track later.

![Pre-Print QR Codes for camp](/screens/tools-utilities/pre-print-qr-codes/PrePrintQR_ForCamp.png)

## Unassigned QR Codes

The **Unassigned** tab shows QR codes that have been generated but are not yet linked to a blood unit.

This tab helps users see which QR codes are still available for future allocation.

![Pre-Print QR Codes unassigned](/screens/tools-utilities/pre-print-qr-codes/PrePrintQR_Unassigned.png)

## Assigned QR Codes

The **Assigned** tab shows QR codes that are already linked to blood units.

This helps staff understand which QR codes have already been used and prevents duplicate assignment.

![Pre-Print QR Codes assigned](/screens/tools-utilities/pre-print-qr-codes/PrePringQr_Assigned.png)

## View Action

When the user clicks the **View** action, a QR preview opens.

This lets the user inspect the QR code before printing and confirm the QR ID visually.

![Pre-Print QR Codes view action](/screens/tools-utilities/pre-print-qr-codes/PrePrintQr_ViewAction.png)

## Print Action

When the user clicks the **Print** action, the page opens a print-friendly QR sheet.

This generates the final printable QR output for label preparation or physical use.

![Pre-Print QR Codes print action](/screens/tools-utilities/pre-print-qr-codes/PrePrintQr_PrintActions.png)

## Important Notes

- Generate QR codes in advance when labels are needed before blood unit assignment.
- Quantity and duplicate count should be checked carefully before generating.
- Unassigned QR codes are available for future allocation.
- Assigned QR codes are already used and should be reviewed only.
- Use **View** to verify a QR code before printing.
- Use **Print** to create the final printable QR sheet.
- The page supports both review and printing of generated QR codes.
