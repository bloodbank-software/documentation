## Pre-Print QR Codes

The Pre-Print QR Codes page is used to generate QR codes in advance before they are assigned to blood units.

This tool helps staff prepare printable QR codes in bulk and review them in a clear list-based view.

![Pre-Print QR Codes overview](/screens/tools-utilities/pre-print-qr-codes/PrePrintQR_Overview.png)

## Overview

Pre-Print QR Codes allows users to create QR labels before blood unit allocation.

The page provides a simple workflow to configure the number of QR codes, review generated entries, preview individual QR codes, and print them when needed.

## Purpose

The main purpose of this page is to support advance QR generation for blood bank operations.

It is useful when QR codes need to be prepared ahead of collection, stock handling, or unit assignment workflows.

## Page Layout

The page is divided into two main parts:

- a configuration section at the top
- a QR list section below it

The configuration section is used to set the QR generation values.

The list section is used to review generated QR codes in separate tabs.

## QR Generation Settings

Before generating QR codes, the user can enter the required configuration details such as:

- quantity
- duplicate count
- camp or source selection, if applicable

These settings determine how many QR codes will be created and how they will be grouped.

![Pre-Print QR Codes print modal](/screens/tools-utilities/pre-print-qr-codes/PrePrintQR_PrintModal.png)

## Unassigned QR Codes

The **Unassigned** tab shows QR codes that have been generated but not yet linked to a blood unit.

This view is useful when QR labels are being prepared in advance and are still waiting for allocation.

The table helps users quickly check:

- QR ID
- generated time
- current status
- assigned state

![Pre-Print QR Codes unassigned list](/screens/tools-utilities/pre-print-qr-codes/PrePrintQR_UnassignedList.png)

## Assigned QR Codes

The **Assigned** tab shows QR codes that are already linked to blood units.

This helps staff track which QR code has already been used and which blood unit it belongs to.

This view is important for avoiding duplicate assignment and for quick verification during operations.

![Pre-Print QR Codes assigned list](/screens/tools-utilities/pre-print-qr-codes/PrePrintQR_AssignedList.png)

## QR Preview

Each QR code can be previewed before printing.

The preview popup shows:

- the QR image
- the QR ID

This allows users to confirm the QR code before sending it to print.

![Pre-Print QR Codes preview](/screens/tools-utilities/pre-print-qr-codes/PrePrintQR_QRPreview.png)

## Print Action

After selecting a QR code, the user can open the print action.

The print flow shows the QR in a print-friendly format so it can be saved or printed directly.

This ensures the QR code is ready for label printing or physical use.

![Pre-Print QR Codes print output](/screens/tools-utilities/pre-print-qr-codes/PrePrintQR_PrintOutput.png)

## Important Notes

- Generated QR codes are available in list form after creation.
- Unassigned and assigned QR codes are shown separately for clarity.
- QR preview should be checked before printing if verification is needed.
- Print layout may vary depending on the browser print settings.
- This page is intended for QR preparation and management only.
