## Overview

Blood Bank is a centralized blood bank management system designed to help staff manage donor records, blood collection, screening, inventory, storage, issue handling, returns, discard flow, and related operational activities in one place.

The system is built to support the complete lifecycle of a blood unit, from the moment a donor is registered or a unit is received from another source, until the unit is screened, stored, issued, returned, discarded, or moved between storage locations. It helps teams maintain accurate records, reduce manual tracking, and manage blood bank operations in a structured and reliable way.

### What the system helps with

Blood Bank provides a clear workflow for handling daily operational tasks such as:

- managing donor details and contact information
- adding blood units from collection or external sources
- tracking screening and testing progress
- storing units in the correct location
- monitoring unit status and movement history
- reserving, issuing, returning, or discarding blood units
- reviewing inventory summaries and blood group availability

### Why it is useful

Blood bank operations require accurate tracking, fast access to information, and careful handling of every blood unit. This system helps teams keep all important information connected in one place so users can work faster, avoid duplicate records, and follow the correct process at every stage.

### Who uses it

The application is intended for blood bank staff who handle:

- donor management
- collection and inventory updates
- laboratory screening
- storage and movement of blood units
- issue and return operations
- reporting and daily operational review

### High-level workflow

In a typical flow, a blood unit is collected or received, linked to the relevant donor or source, assigned a QR code, screened in the laboratory, and then moved into the appropriate storage location. From there, it can later be reserved, issued, returned, transferred, or discarded based on the actual operational status of the unit.

This documentation explains the Blood Bank system from an end-user perspective and focuses on how to use the available screens and workflows effectively.
