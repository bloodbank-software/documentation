## Key Features

### Donor Management
- Add and manage donor records in a structured way.
- Store donor profile details, contact information, blood group, and related history.
- Track donor-related activities across the system.

### Blood Collection and QR Tracking
- Register blood units collected from donors or received from other sources.
- Assign QR codes to blood units for easy identification and tracking.
- Use QR-based lookup to manage units quickly and accurately.

### Inventory and Storage Management
- Track available blood units across different blood groups.
- Store units in the correct building, zone, and equipment.
- View current stock, storage status, and unit movement clearly.

### Lab Testing and Screening
- Record mandatory screening results for blood units.
- Monitor whether tests are pending, in progress, safe, or failed.
- Move units through the correct screening and storage workflow.

### Reservation, Issue, and Return
- Reserve blood units for patient requirements.
- Issue units when needed and track issue history.
- Handle returned units and re-testing before reuse.

### Discard and Expiry Handling
- Identify expired, failed, damaged, or unusable blood units.
- Move such units into discard flow with proper reason tracking.
- Keep discarded units separate from active inventory.

### Communication and Follow-Up
- Maintain donor communication history in one place.
- Track phone follow-ups, responses, and remarks.
- Support better donor engagement and re-contact workflows.

### Settings and Master Data
- Manage supporting records such as locations, reasons, and storage data.
- Keep system values configurable from one place.
- Use master data across different workflows consistently.

### Dashboard and Reporting
- View quick summaries of blood stock and unit status.
- Check key counts and operational visibility from dashboard views.
- Use reports for day-to-day monitoring and review.
