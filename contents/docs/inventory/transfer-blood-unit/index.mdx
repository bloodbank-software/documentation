title: Transfer Blood Unit
description: Transfer safe stored blood units to another destination from inventory.
---

The **Transfer Blood Unit** workflow is used to move a blood unit from inventory to another destination, such as a hospital or another blood bank.

This action is connected to the blood unit details page and is visible only when the unit is in a suitable safe stored state.

## Overview

Transfer is used when a blood unit leaves the current inventory and needs to be recorded as moved to another place.

## When Transfer Is Available

The transfer button appears only for screened-safe stored blood units.

![Inventory transfer button for safe stored units](/screens/inventory/Inventory_Blood_TransferButton_onlyfor_screenedSafe_storedbloodunits.png)

### What the User Sees

- the blood unit details page
- a Transfer button
- current unit status
- unit history and audit trail

### How to Start a Transfer

1. Open the blood unit details page.
2. Confirm that the unit is screened safe and stored.
3. Click **Transfer**.
4. Complete the transfer details in the form that opens.

## Transfer Flow

After the transfer action is selected, the user enters the destination and handling details.

![Inventory blood unit detail page - screening safe status](/screens/inventory/Inventory_Blood_DetailPage_ScreeningSafeStatus.png)

### Transfer Details to Enter

- destination type
- destination name
- transfer reason
- transfer date and time
- remarks or handling instructions

### Important Note

Transfer is allowed only when the blood unit meets the required safe and stored conditions.

## Important Notes

- Always verify the destination before confirming the transfer.
- Make sure the transfer reason is clear and accurate.
- Use transfer only for eligible units.
- The transfer record should match the inventory movement history.
